  Compatibility and logic &
  \path{PvsNP/Compatibility.lean} checks descent, equality reflection
  from a left inverse, and tracked substitution from its coding law.
  The diagonal lemma, incompleteness, and completeness are cited. \\
  \addlinespace
  CVP and affine selection &
  \path{HCP/CoupledSignResidual.lean} checks the cost and gap.
  Python computes the Paper~1 residual and selects from the explicit
  affine system. A proof of the implementation's full-fiber
  specification remains a target. \\
  \addlinespace
  Affine disclosure &
  \path{PvsNP/AffineDisclosure.lean} checks the pointwise equivalences,
  commuting identity, and diagonal. The coordinate rings, first-colon
  quotient, counts, and choice of coordinates follow by the written arguments. \\
  \addlinespace
  Polynomial agreement certificates &
  \path{PvsNP/AgreementCertificate.lean} checks the affine comparison
  for normalized coefficients. \path{python/polynomial_agreement_selector.py}
  selects certificates over a prime field and checks the four-site examples. \\
  \addlinespace
  Finite Boolean agreement &
  Section~\ref{sec:boolean-agreement} proves the projection formula and
  identifies agreement with the tagged cross component.
  \path{python/agreement_scheme.py} checks the two one-bit families by symbolic
  elimination and exhaustive evaluation. \\
  \addlinespace
  Orbit closures and finite symmetry &
  \path{PvsNP/OrbitClosureSeparation.lean} checks the rank-one separator.
  \path{PvsNP/FiniteSymmetry.lean} checks the finite commutant and its
  dimension. The graded comparison uses complete reducibility. \\
  \addlinespace
  Permanent level spaces &
  Section~\ref{sec:gct} gives the coefficient pullback and the tangent
  Hessian bound. \path{python/gct_permanent_fiber.py} checks the symmetric
  rank-two pencil, its support bound, and the Hessian rank at \(J_4\). \\
  \addlinespace
  Three-plane constraints &
  Section~\ref{sec:gct-three-plane} gives the written maximality, rank,
  and symmetry arguments and proves coverage by four representative Witt
  charts. Python checks the existing order-four identities and chart
  coordinates, the modular rank certificate for the zero-row-sum
  exclusion, and the three-cycle eigenspace coefficients and fixed
  sector. It also verifies the ordinary-diagonal identities and
  extension kernels. \\
  \addlinespace
  Modular obstructions &
  Section~\ref{sec:gct-modular} gives the written specialization and
  chart reductions. \path{python/gct_permanent_modular.py} checks rational
  points at 5 and 7 and the unit ideals for the two rank exclusions
  at 11 and 23. \path{python/gct_permanent_involutions.py} checks the
  involution chart ideals at 11, and \path{gct_permanent_f25.cpp} checks
  the three extension-field charts. The cubic-field orbit bound is a
  written argument. \\
  \addlinespace
  Extending a level two-plane &
  \path{PvsNP/LevelSetExtension.lean} checks polynomial first variation
  and the hyperplane reduction. Section~\ref{sec:gct-extension} gives
  the dimension criterion and specialization argument.
  \path{python/gct_permanent_extensions.py} checks the 156 prime-field two-planes
  and their residual equations. \\
  \addlinespace
  Pl\"ucker cuts &
  Section~\ref{sec:gct-plucker} gives the extension-field example,
  the single-edge exclusion, and the 24-chart cover.
  \path{python/gct_permanent_plucker.py} checks the example and generates
  the exact msolve inputs. Section~\ref{sec:gct-path} reduces the
  unrestricted problem to a single path chart using the checked
  opposite-edge systems. Exact bases for the matching and adjacent
  cases exclude any direction on at most two edges. The triangle
  calculation gives the single star chart, and properness gives
  finiteness. \path{PvsNP/LevelSetExtension.lean} verifies coordinate
  selection conditional on the four-coordinate projection being
  injective. The generator checks the
  signs of the path-reversal equations, and the written averaging
  argument restricts certificate search to even products. Checking the
  stored functional on all even products excludes degree-seven
  identities for these generators. The four-cycle chart omitting
  frequency two has a separate verified degree-eight bound. \\
  \addlinespace
  Frobenius orbits &
  \path{python/gct_permanent_frobenius.py} checks the five twisted Witt
  searches and the double-transposition fixed sector.
  Section~\ref{sec:gct-path} derives the 48-point lower bounds and
  the invariant-algebra criterion. \path{python/gct_permanent_plucker.py}
  computes the generators for the multiplication checks. \\
  \addlinespace
  Invariant closure &
  \path{PvsNP/InvariantClosure.lean} proves the abstract
  finite dimension bound. \path{python/gct_permanent_closure.py}
  checks supplied product identities and replays the degree-seven
  obstruction. Section~\ref{sec:gct-path} proves the leading
  boundary description and the center-diagonal rewrites. \\
  \addlinespace
  Hodge collision branches &
  \path{python/gct_permanent_plucker.py} verifies the edge form and
  exports the two branches. \path{python/gct_permanent_hodge.py}
  generates the Witt chart whose exact reduced basis is \([1]\)
  and the 17-variable collision system.
  Its edge-family systems test the transverse pencil and the
  possible leaf symmetries of the edge image.
  Section~\ref{sec:gct-path} supplies chart coverage and specialization. \\
  \addlinespace
  Local readout algebra &
  \path{PvsNP/PermanentReadout.lean} parametrizes kernel differences
  at a unit minor. \path{PvsNP/ReadoutPivotIdeal.lean} computes the
  first colon of the three readout equations.
  \path{PvsNP/ReadoutResidual.lean} checks the punctured-family
  image kernel and base change.
  \path{PvsNP/OrientationDescent.lean} constructs the complementary
  projectors and their coefficientwise Frobenius action. The field-degree
  bound takes the degree-two exclusion as an input. \\
  \addlinespace
  Edge charts &
  \path{PvsNP/EdgeDiscriminant.lean} checks the scalar and transverse
  Gram determinants. \path{PvsNP/ScalarOrientation.lean} applies the
  quadratic valuation obstruction and gives both normalized roots at
  a double zero. For the fixed transverse pair
  \path{PvsNP/TransversePairLevel.lean} proves that the permanent
  differs from \(24\) using the coefficient certificate.
  \path{PvsNP/EdgeStabilizer.lean} proves the orbit
  sizes from the two geometric symmetry exclusions. \\
  \addlinespace
  Complexity comparisons &
  Proof translation and polynomial boundedness use Cook--Reckhow.
  The quotient \(B/R\) follows directly from the predicate definitions. \\
\end{longtable}
